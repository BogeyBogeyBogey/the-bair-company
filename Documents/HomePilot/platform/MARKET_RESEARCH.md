# Market Research: Property Intelligence For Renovation Opportunities

Research date: 2026-06-19.
Current-source refresh: 2026-06-19.

HomePilot should not be positioned as a simple lead generator. The stronger
category is a property intelligence and campaign learning platform for
renovation companies: property records, opportunity scores, visual evidence,
campaign contact history, responses, non-responses, exports, and private
tenant learnings in one controlled workspace.

## Comparable Systems

### Property Data Foundations

These platforms sell trusted property facts, coverage, APIs, and analytics.

- ATTOM positions itself around property data, ownership, permits, boundaries,
  valuation, risk, API/bulk/cloud delivery, and data quality transparency.
  Source: https://www.attomdata.com/
- Reonomy positions machine learning around a clean property/owner record,
  ownership resolution, predictive scoring, property details, transaction
  history, web app access, and API/data delivery.
  Source: https://www.reonomy.com/
- PropStream combines nationwide property records, AI-assisted lead discovery,
  many filters and lead lists, skip tracing, campaign tools, comps,
  calculators, demographic insights, teams, and mobile access.
  Source: https://www.propstream.com/
- Regrid focuses on parcel boundaries, buildings, addresses, ownership,
  zoning, geographic enrichments, APIs, files, tiles, and Esri-compatible
  enterprise delivery.
  Source: https://regrid.com/
- Cotality/CoreLogic brings broad property, climate, risk, valuation,
  underwriting, claims, roof-age, roof-condition, and portfolio intelligence.
  Source: https://www.cotality.com/

Best practices to adopt:

- Coverage and freshness should be visible, not implied.
- Every score needs provenance: source, timestamp, confidence, and evidence.
- Enterprise buyers expect APIs, bulk exports, cloud delivery, and auditability.
- Data quality transparency is a product feature, not back-office hygiene.

HomePilot advantage:

- These platforms are broad data suppliers. HomePilot can be narrower and more
  actionable: renovate-this-property, contact-this-segment, learn-from-this-
  response.

### Aerial, Visual, And Measurement Intelligence

These platforms turn imagery into reliable property inspection and measurement
workflows.

- Nearmap emphasizes current high-resolution imagery, proprietary AI, many
  semantic property layers, workflow apps, integrations, and APIs.
  Source: https://www.nearmap.com/
- Eagleview combines high-resolution aerial imagery, property data, roof/wall/
  window/door reports, 3D visualizations, measurements, developer APIs, and
  integrations with contractor systems.
  Source: https://www.eagleview.com/
- Hover converts a property scan into a 3D model, measurements, real-material
  designs, estimates, proposals, and production-ready material lists.
  Source: https://hover.to/

Best practices to adopt:

- Visual proof matters: customers need to see why a property was scored.
- Measurements and evidence should be near the score, not hidden in raw files.
- The strongest products bridge inspection, design, estimate, and proposal.
- Integrations with CRM/estimating tools are table stakes for large accounts.

HomePilot advantage:

- These tools usually start from a known job or inspection. HomePilot can start
  earlier: find renovation opportunities across a territory before the customer
  ever asks for a quote.

### Contractor Workflow And CRM

These systems own the operational workflow after a lead exists.

- Roofr combines CRM, lead capture, instant estimates, measurement reports,
  proposals, material ordering, invoicing, payments, calendar, and mobile use.
  Source: https://roofr.com/
- ServiceTitan shows the enterprise contractor suite expectation: CRM,
  proposal builder, reporting dashboards, service agreements, accounting,
  marketing, booking, estimates, dispatching, mobile workflows, invoicing,
  project management, and customer portal.
  Source: https://www.servicetitan.com/
- JobNimbus focuses on contractor CRM, sales boards, measurements, mobile work,
  insights, automations, estimates, communications, production, billing, and
  integrations.
  Source: https://www.jobnimbus.com/
- AccuLynx is another roofing/exterior contractor platform category reference,
  especially for CRM, production, material ordering, and partner integrations.
  Source: https://acculynx.com/

Best practices to adopt:

- Leads must flow into a clear sales process: status, owner, next action, value.
- Kanban/pipeline views are useful, but enterprise customers also need exports.
- Automations and reminders are expected once campaign response data exists.
- Mobile-friendly field review becomes important once sales teams start using
  the data daily.

HomePilot advantage:

- CRMs manage known opportunities. HomePilot can feed them higher-quality,
  evidence-backed opportunities and explain why each property deserves action.

### Lead Marketplaces And Compliance Lessons

Lead marketplaces prove demand but also show the reputational risk of weak lead
claims.

- The FTC finalized an order against HomeAdvisor/Angi related to deceptive lead
  marketing claims, including claims about lead quality, source, and readiness
  to hire.
  Source: https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2023/04/ftc-approves-final-order-against-homeadvisor-inc-deceptively-marketing-its-leads-home-improvement

Best practices to adopt:

- Never claim that a property owner is ready to buy unless that is evidenced.
- Separate opportunity score from intent score.
- Store source and consent/provenance for every contactable record.
- Track no-response honestly; it is learning data, not a failure to hide.
- Keep opt-outs, do-not-contact states, and export logs first-class.

HomePilot advantage:

- HomePilot can avoid the marketplace trap by selling intelligence and campaign
  learning, not vague lead intent. A property can be a high renovation
  opportunity without claiming homeowner buying intent.

## Product Rules To Copy

1. Data must be inspectable.
   Show address, map position, module scores, confidence, evidence, campaign
   status, and export readiness together.

2. Every customer handoff must be scoped.
   The source payload, dashboard, export, manifest, audit report, and export log
   must prove tenant and module boundaries.

3. The platform needs two modes.
   Spreadsheet mode for daily work and impressive visual mode for strategy,
   learning, territory analysis, and executive review.

4. AI scores need receipts.
   A score without evidence, timestamp, confidence, and metric breakdown will
   not survive enterprise review.

5. Learnings must be privacy-safe.
   Raw reactions, addresses, and notes stay tenant-private. Cross-customer
   benchmarks must be aggregate-only, thresholded, and identifier-free.

6. Renovation modules must share the same property brain.
   WindowPilot, FacadePilot, RoofPilot, GardenPilot, PoolPilot, PorchPilot, and
   DrivewayPilot should enrich the same property object, not create fragmented
   databases.

7. The platform should be CRM-adjacent, not a CRM clone.
   HomePilot should push clean opportunities, next actions, and response
   learnings into contractor systems instead of rebuilding every downstream
   production workflow first.

## Where HomePilot Can Be Better

- Multi-renovation intelligence: most comparable systems are either broad data
  suppliers, visual measurement tools, or single-trade CRMs. HomePilot can show
  one house as a stacked renovation opportunity across facade, windows, roof,
  garden, pool, porch, and driveway.
- Campaign learning loop: track contacted/not contacted, responded/no response,
  objections, variant, channel, and next action per property and module.
- Second-brain visuals: graph properties, signals, modules, neighborhoods,
  campaigns, objections, and benchmark cohorts so large customers see why the
  system is learning.
- Customer-safe evidence pack: combine dashboard, scoped payload, Excel export,
  access audit, data quality report, export log, and readiness evidence.
- European/local wedge: start with highly practical regional renovation
  targeting rather than trying to be a generic U.S.-style property data vendor.
- Honest lead language: position records as opportunity intelligence unless the
  owner actually responded or consented.

## Market-Ready Requirements

Already represented in the platform:

- Shared `HomePilot/platform` layer instead of per-pilot databases.
- Canonical tenants, modules, campaigns, properties, assessments,
  interactions, exports, and benchmarks.
- Tenant/module scoped customer packages with `scoped_payload.json`.
- Deployable customer portal bundles with dashboard assets, tenant exports,
  live Auth/RLS runtime config, security headers, redirects, route map, and
  secret scan evidence.
- Portal hosting evidence packs with asset manifests, cache policy, provider
  configs, rollback manifests, private-access guardrails, and hosted production
  blockers.
- CRM/webhook integration handoff packs with sales import CSV, JSONL webhook
  payloads, provider field mappings, idempotency/retry contract, and secret scan.
- CRM/webhook sync runner with dry-run evidence, env-only credentials,
  idempotency, retry accounting, dead-letter output, and secret-safe reports.
- Monitoring/alerting packs with customer-safe owners, cadence, source gates,
  production blockers, alert conditions, remediation steps, and alert matrix.
- Visual intelligence scale packs with clustered map models, second-brain graph
  render budgets, boardroom hubs, and map cluster exports for large territories.
- Data vendor/enrichment readiness packs for parcel, geocode, imagery, energy,
  permit, pricing, and contact-provenance coverage plus per-property backlog.
- Data vendor refresh runner with dry-run evidence, env-only credentials,
  idempotency, retry accounting, dead-letter output, and secret-safe reports.
- Synthetic all-module enterprise demo room with dashboard, exports, portal,
  CRM handoff, enrichment plan, data dictionary, access audit, and audit trail.
- Reviewable account access packs for invitees, roles, permissions,
  membership SQL, and revocation SQL before live customer access.
- Customer access verification contracts that turn planned invitees into
  redacted live RLS probe identities without writing tokens or passwords.
- Data quality audit for score, geocode, evidence, target, response, and
  duplicate coverage.
- Privacy-safe aggregate benchmarks with minimum cohort thresholds.
- Readiness pack that refuses to claim production until live RLS proof exists.
- CSV/XLSX exports and static dashboard handoff.
- Customer package source ledger for evidence coverage, source runs,
  confidence, timestamps, provenance gaps, and safe lead-claim guardrails.

Still needed before a true market-ready claim:

- Live Supabase launch with real credentials and `production_verified: true`.
- Customer-approved or live multi-module demo data for named verticals once
  production contracts and data processing terms are signed.
- Live execution of production authentication, account invitations, role
  provisioning, and customer access verification with real Supabase Auth users.
- Customer-approved production hosting URL, private/access-controlled hosting
  proof, real customer auth sessions, configured public runtime keys, and live
  RLS-backed reads.
- Production map-tile provider selection and hosted map styling for live
  territories beyond the current dependency-free clustered dashboard view.
- Customer-specific CRM/webhook credentials, live production sync runs, and
  live customer alert routing/escalation destinations.
- Compliance pack for contact provenance, opt-outs, retention, consent, and
  lead-claim language.
- Live vendor/API contracts, customer-approved endpoint credentials, and
  production refresh runs for parcels, imagery, permits, energy/age signals,
  and pricing/estimate enrichments.

## Recommended Positioning

HomePilot is the customer-safe property intelligence layer for renovation
growth teams. It finds renovation opportunities, explains the evidence, tracks
outreach and response learning, and turns every contacted property into a
private, exportable, visual knowledge base for the customer.
