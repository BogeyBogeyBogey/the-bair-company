Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 July 2026

The Bair Co., established in Glabbeek, Belgium (enterprise number BE 1034.232.509, e-mail kristof@baircompany.be), is the controller for the processing of personal data described in this policy, in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. What data we process

We only process personal data you provide or that is generated through your use of this website:

  • Contact data — name, e-mail address, company name and the content of your message when you use the contact form or e-mail us.
  • Usage data — anonymised analytics data (pages visited, approximate location, device type) via Google Analytics 4, only after your consent.
  • Preferences — your language choice and cookie preferences, stored locally in your browser.

2. Why we process it

  • To respond to your enquiry (legal basis: pre-contractual measures / legitimate interest).
  • To improve our website and services (legal basis: consent, via analytics cookies).
  • To comply with legal obligations, e.g. accounting (legal basis: legal obligation).

3. Processors and transfers

We use a limited set of processors: Formspree (contact form handling), Vercel (website hosting) and Google (analytics, after consent). Where these providers process data outside the EEA, transfers are covered by adequacy decisions (such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework) or standard contractual clauses. We never sell personal data.

4. Retention

Contact enquiries are kept for as long as needed to handle your request, and up to 24 months afterwards for follow-up. Analytics data is retained per Google Analytics settings (14 months). Accounting records follow statutory retention periods.

5. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase and port your personal data, to restrict or object to processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. Exercise these rights via kristof@baircompany.be. We respond within 30 days.

You can also lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit), Drukpersstraat 35, 1000 Brussels — gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit.be.

6. Security

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures: encrypted connections (HTTPS/HSTS), strict security headers, access control and data minimisation.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The most recent version always applies and is published on this page.
