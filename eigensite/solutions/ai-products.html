CFAQ

Common questions.

How long does it take to build an AI product?
A working first version typically takes 6–12 weeks, depending on scope. We ship in short cycles, so you see working software from week one — not a big reveal at the end.
Which AI models and stack do you use?
We're model-agnostic. We select per use case — frontier APIs, open-weight models or a hybrid — based on quality, latency, privacy and cost. The architecture keeps you free to switch as the market moves.
Who owns the product and the code?
You do. Full source code, infrastructure and documentation are handed over — no lock-in, no licensing games.
