CFAQ

Common questions.

Which AI regulations apply to us?
That depends on where you operate, what the system does and who it affects. We map the relevant jurisdictions, classify your use cases and translate obligations into a concrete, proportionate action list.
We already ran pilots. Why didn't they stick?
The usual causes: no owner, no integration with real workflows, no training, no measurement. A pilot without an adoption plan is a demo. We design for the day after the pilot.
Can you work alongside our internal IT or existing vendors?
Yes. We regularly operate as the AI specialist inside a larger landscape, integrating with internal teams, system integrators and security officers rather than around them.
