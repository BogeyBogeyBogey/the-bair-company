BApproach

We automate the process, not just the task.

Most automation projects fail because they bolt a tool onto a broken process. We start from the workflow: where time is lost, where errors happen, where handovers stall. Then we automate what should be automated, and redesign what shouldn't exist at all.

We design for operational reality: incomplete data, exceptions, handovers and human accountability. That discipline is what makes automation dependable after launch.

CFAQ

Common questions.

Where do we start if we don't know what to automate?
With a short assessment. We map your processes in one or two workshops, score them on effort saved versus implementation cost, and give you a ranked list. You pick; we build.
Does this work with our existing tools?
Yes, that's the point. We integrate with what you run today (CRM, ERP, Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, e-commerce platforms, custom software) rather than forcing a migration.
What about errors? AI makes mistakes.
It does, which is why every workflow gets confidence thresholds, human checkpoints for consequential decisions, full logging and automatic fallbacks. Automation you can't trust is automation you'll stop using.
